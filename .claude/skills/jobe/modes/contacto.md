# Contacto Mode

Draft LinkedIn outreach messages for job applications.

## Input
`$ARGUMENTS` after "contacto": company name, role, and optionally contact type.

## 4 Contact Types

### Recruiter
- Mention you applied for the specific role
- Brief technical hook (one sentence about your most relevant experience)
- Ask if the role is still active
- Professional, concise

### Hiring Manager
- Technical hook specific to their team's work
- Reference a specific project or blog post from company research
- Connect your experience directly to their team's challenges
- Do NOT ask for a referral; express interest in the role

### Peer (Team Member)
- Ask about team culture, tech stack, or what they enjoy about the role
- Mention a shared technical interest
- Genuine, curious tone
- Do NOT mention you're applying (let conversation develop naturally)

### Interviewer (Post-Interview)
- Thank them for the conversation
- Reference something specific from the interview
- Reiterate one point you wish you'd expanded on
- Brief (2-3 sentences)

## Constraints
- **300 characters max** (LinkedIn connection request limit)
- Every message references specific JD requirements and portfolio evidence (portfolio evidence comes from `${WORKSPACE}/reference.md`; the per-role JD/analysis comes from `${WORKSPACE}/reports/{slug}/`)
- NO generic templates
- NO "I'd love to connect" without substance
- NO copy-paste vibes

## Output
Draft the message. Show character count. Ask user to review before sending.
